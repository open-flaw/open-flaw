## open-flaw - Open Security Research & Public PoC Repository

**Our Mission:** To advance the state of cybersecurity through transparent, community-driven vulnerability research.

**What We Do:**
*   **Security Research:** We proactively hunt for critical vulnerabilities in widely-used software.
*   **Public PoCs:** We provide detailed, open-source Proof of Concept (PoC) code to help the community understand and validate newly disclosed CVEs.
*   **Educational Resources:** We document our findings and methodologies to empower the next generation of security professionals.

**Get Involved:** We believe the security of open-source software is a shared responsibility. Whether you're a seasoned researcher or just starting, your contributions matter. Explore our repositories, submit your own PoCs, and join us in making the digital world a safer place.
